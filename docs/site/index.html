Why Pangan?

Named after an ancient water clock

Pangan (پنگان) is the Persian word for an ancient water clock used to divide time fairly among people. A fitting ancestor for a focus timer — small, precise, and fair to your attention.

Why Rust? Pangan is a native application, not a browser tab. It starts fast, stays quiet, and respects your system resources — with a single, portable binary on desktop.

Why open source? Your focus data belongs to you. Pangan is MIT-licensed, runs fully offline, and is transparent about what it does.
